About Us

Specialized hair restoration for Afro-textured hair since 2015.

Artistic Hair Transplant Centre is a modern, specialized clinic in Nairobi, Kenya since 2015, dedicated to advanced hair restoration for men and women with Afro-textured hair.

Our team has extensive experience working with tightly-coiled, curly, and kinky hair types, ensuring every procedure respects the unique structure, curl pattern, and follicular characteristics of African hair.

We provide exceptional Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP) and FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) services, each tailored to the individual needs of clients seeking natural-looking density, refined hairlines, and long-lasting results.

Our facility utilizes advanced, minimally invasive techniques designed specifically to protect the curvature of Afro hair follicles — allowing patients to achieve excellent cosmetic outcomes with minimal risk and downtime.

By combining the latest FUE technologies with expertly executed SMP sessions for clients who prefer non-surgical solutions, our highly trained trichology team delivers leading-edge treatments in a comfortable, supportive, and culturally aware environment.

We offer a client-centered approach to correcting hair loss, thinning, traction alopecia, and male or female pattern baldness with precision to minimize scarring, maximize density, and blend seamlessly with natural Afro-textured hair.
